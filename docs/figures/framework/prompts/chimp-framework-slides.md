# Slide prompts — "The Analyst Inside" (a companion to *Safe-TRE Agent*)

A set of storybook explainer slides in the **same house style** as the existing
`Safe-TRE Agent` slides (the Guide, the Raven, the Keep, Lean the owl, Alloy the
brass tracker). Feed this whole file to the image model. Generate **one image per
"SLIDE" below**, and keep the cast, palette and framing identical across all of
them so they read as one deck.

---

## HOUSE STYLE (applies to every slide)

- **Format:** wide 16:9 landscape illustration, a single finished slide.
- **Medium:** warm hand-painted anime / storybook watercolour, cel-like outlines,
  soft gouache shading, bright but muted natural colours. Cosy, reassuring, a
  little wondrous — never dark or menacing.
- **Ground:** aged cream **parchment**, faint paper grain.
- **Border:** an ornate thin **gold** frame with scrollwork corner flourishes and
  small four-point star/sparkle accents; a couple of little **navy heraldic
  shields** (gold star, gold padlock, gold gear emblems) tucked in the top
  corners, laurel-wreathed.
- **Type on the slide:** a large **navy (deep indigo) serif** title across the top,
  centred; a short **navy italic** subtitle beneath it; character and object
  **labels in little parchment banners** with a navy pin-star; one **motto banner**
  low-centre in navy; and along the very bottom a **numbered flow strip** —
  small circled numbers and simple icons joined by arrows.
- **Palette:** parchment cream, deep navy/indigo, antique gold, with sage-green,
  slate-blue and warm brown accents. Keep it consistent slide to slide.

## THE CAST (draw these the same every time)

- **Researcher** — a friendly young adventurer-scholar, tousled brown hair, a
  travel cloak and a satchel of instruments. Stands *outside* the keep.
- **The Keep (the TRE)** — a stout grey **castle** on a rocky river bluff, blue
  conical roofs, hung with navy banners bearing gold **padlock**, **scales** and
  **people** emblems; a heavy padlocked gate. This is the trusted data haven.
- **The Guide** *(returning, minor)* — a cheerful green-haired elf-mage in blue
  and white robes, half-moon glasses; the outside translator. Appears small, at
  the wall, in the title and errand slides only.
- **The Raven** *(returning, minor)* — a glossy black messenger raven with a
  little navy collar, carrying a wax-sealed envelope. The thin link across the wall.
- **CHIMP (the new star)** — a clever, endearing **chimpanzee scholar**: little
  navy-and-gold waistcoat, half-moon spectacles, alert intelligent eyes, *friendly
  not fierce*. It lives **inside a glass-and-brass enclosure** within the keep. It
  is plainly capable but plainly contained — a caged genius, happy at its desk.
- **The Scribe** — a small tidy **owl-like clerk** (cousin to Lean) outside the
  wall, spectacled, writing a neat report from slips that came back through the gate.
- **The Castellan** *(operator)* — a calm elder keeper in navy robes with a big
  brass key ring at the belt; appears only in the last slide.

---

## SLIDE 1 — TITLE

**On-slide title:** `The Analyst Inside`
**Subtitle:** `Last time, the Guide stayed outside. Now meet Chimp.`

Scene: the same Keep at centre-right on its river bluff, banners flying. On the
LEFT, the **Researcher** waves. Beside the keep, the small **Guide** and the
**Raven** stand at the padlocked gate as before. But now a **cutaway window** in
the keep wall reveals **CHIMP** inside its glass-and-brass enclosure at a desk of
instruments — the new resident. A soft golden light glows from inside. Character
banners: `Researcher`, `Guide`, `TRE`, `Chimp`.
**Bottom flow strip:** `1 Ask  →  2 Plan  →  3 Check  →  4 Answer`.

## SLIDE 2 — MEET CHIMP

**Title:** `Chimp, in the Enclosure`
**Subtitle:** `Clever enough to help. Caged so it need not be trusted.`

Scene: interior of the keep. **CHIMP** sits contentedly inside a **glass-and-brass
enclosure**, at a wooden desk. Around it, a small **fixed rack of labelled
instruments** — an abacus, a set-square, a little bell-curve chart, a tiny
hourglass-clock — its only tools. Behind, faintly glowing **stacked crates of
sealed diaries** marked with a padlock (the data), safely out of reach. Chimp
reads a slip through the glass; its scribbled notes drift toward the enclosure
wall and stop dead, turning to little puffs. Warm lantern light. Labels:
`Chimp — the inside analyst`, `The Enclosure`, `Data (sealed)`.
**Motto banner:** `Clever, and kept.`
**Bottom flow strip:** `1 Ask  →  2 Plan  →  3 Check  →  4 Answer` (highlight 2).

## SLIDE 3 — A WHOLE ERRAND, ONE GATE

**Title:** `A Whole Question, One Gate`
**Subtitle:** `Chimp runs many analyses inside. Only vetted summaries cross.`

Scene: split by the glowing **gateway wall** down the middle. LEFT, outside: the
**Researcher** hands a large scroll (a whole research *question*, not a single
form) to the **Raven**, which carries it through the small slot. RIGHT, inside the
enclosure: **CHIMP** busily works through a little stack of question-slips at its
desk (many analyses), then seals a tidy **dossier** with a wax stamp. The Raven
carries only that sealed dossier back out to the **Scribe**, who writes a neat
plain-language report from it. Several of Chimp's slips bear a small red
**"refused"** stamp — the keep said no, and Chimp kept those inside. Labels:
`a whole question`, `Chimp works inside`, `the dossier`, `Scribe`.
**Motto banner:** `Only vetted summaries cross the wall.`
**Bottom flow strip:** `1 Ask  →  2 Plan  →  3 Check  →  4 Answer` (highlight 3).

## SLIDE 4 — THE MENU ON THE WALL

**Title:** `A Language You Can Read on One Page`
**Subtitle:** `Chimp can only ever speak in a few fixed sentences.`

Scene: inside the enclosure, a grand **menu board / illuminated spellbook** on the
wall lists **four fixed question-templates**, each as a fill-in-the-blank sentence
with glowing blank slots: *"the average of ▢ by ▢"*, *"the correlation of ▢ and ▢"*,
*"a regression of ▢ on ▢"*, *"a time series of ▢ by ▢"*. **CHIMP** points a
teacher's cane at one, choosing. To one side, three items are **crossed out with a
red X** on little tags: *"write code"*, *"read one person's record"*, *"invent a
new question"* — the things it cannot do. Labels: `The Menu`, `— and no other words —`.
**Motto banner:** `A fixed menu. No sixth kind of sentence.`
**Bottom flow strip:** `1 Ask  →  2 Plan  →  3 Check  →  4 Answer` (highlight 2).

## SLIDE 5 — THE SEALED PLAN

**Title:** `Write It Down Before You Look`
**Subtitle:** `Even Chimp's clever choices are sealed in the logbook first.`

Scene: inside the enclosure. **CHIMP** writes a **plan** onto a scroll and presses
a **wax seal** into a big, chained, iron-bound **logbook** *before* turning to the
work — the plan is committed and locked. Beside it, a plain **clockwork golem**
(not Chimp) actually runs the sealed plan, cranking a lever. On the desk sits a
small **brass token-jar** with just a few glowing coins — its "peek budget"; a
tiny gauge shows one coin being spent for a single allowed glance at what the
keep withheld, and the jar nearly empty means "no more peeking". Labels:
`the sealed plan`, `a plain machine runs it`, `the peek budget`.
**Motto banner:** `Seal the plan first. Pay for each peek.`
**Bottom flow strip:** `1 Ask  →  2 Plan  →  3 Check  →  4 Answer` (highlight 2 and 3).

## SLIDE 6 — THE WINDOW

**Title:** `You Speak Through a Window`
**Subtitle:** `The keeper decides if Chimp is inside. You only get to ask.`

Scene: the outside of the keep wall, where a small arched **speaking-window** with
a brass grille lets the **Researcher** call a question through to Chimp within
(seen faintly glowing inside). Standing by the window, the **Castellan** (operator)
holds a **big brass key ring** and a small sign that reads `Analyst: ON / OFF` —
turned to `ON` — showing that whether an analyst lives inside at all is the
keeper's setting, fixed at the gate, not the visitor's. Through the window only a
sealed **dossier** passes back out; Chimp, its notes and the diaries stay within.
Labels: `Researcher (asks)`, `the window`, `Castellan (decides)`, `Chimp (inside)`.
**Motto banner:** `The operator decides. The browser only asks.`
**Bottom flow strip:** `1 Ask  →  2 Plan  →  3 Check  →  4 Answer` (highlight 1 and 4).

---

*Tip: if the model garbles the on-slide text, that's expected — regenerate a slide
or two, or keep the labels/mottos short. The four-step bottom flow
(Ask · Plan · Check · Answer) and the parchment-and-gold frame are what tie the
deck together, so keep those identical on every slide.*
